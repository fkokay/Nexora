## Payroll policy decisions and remaining acceptance gates

Türkiye is the confirmed jurisdiction. Owner decisions: monthly payroll periods follow calendar months; supported compensation categories are monthly, daily, and hourly; employees use company-defined categories; overtime, benefits, and deductions are configured through effective-dated company rules and calculations consume only approved attendance; first outputs are payslips and accounting vouchers; each payroll line rounds to two decimals. These choices do not establish statutory correctness. Company-defined rules and internal previews are available, but a verified legal gross/net calculation, accepted statutory declaration payloads, and actual mapped general-ledger voucher posting are not complete. The 2026 candidate year remains disabled for official payroll until an accountant-approved expected-result set and legal review are recorded. Statutory rules must remain effective-dated, versioned, sourced, approved, and captured in reproducible calculation snapshots. Employee and leave/attendance records remain tenant-scoped and minimize sensitive personal data.
